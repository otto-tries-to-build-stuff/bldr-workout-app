1Workout builder — three visual directions

Android, minimal & clean. Each direction shows the three core screens: Home (workouts you've built), Library (browse by muscle/type), and Workout (execution). Execution adapts to the workout — 1a shows the rep-based checklist mode, 1b and 1c show the timed mode. Every workout links to a demo video.
